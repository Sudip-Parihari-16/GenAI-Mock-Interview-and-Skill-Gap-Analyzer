# 🤖 Gen AI Mock Interview and Skill Gap Analyzer

An AI-powered platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for technical interviews, evaluate their skills, identify knowledge gaps, and improve their interview performance.

## 📌 About The Project

The **Gen AI Mock Interview and Skill Gap Analyzer** is a web-based application that uses Generative AI to simulate real-world interview experiences.

The platform helps users practice interview questions, evaluate their responses, identify areas where they need improvement, and receive recommendations to enhance their technical knowledge.

The main objective is to make interview preparation smarter, more personalized, and accessible.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Mock Interviews:** Practice interview questions with an AI-based interview system.
- **Personalized Interview Preparation:** Prepare for interviews based on relevant skills and topics.
- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Identify strengths and weaknesses in your technical knowledge.
- **Performance Evaluation:** Analyze interview responses and understand areas for improvement.
- **AI-Generated Feedback:** Receive intelligent feedback to improve interview answers.
- **Learning Recommendations:** Identify topics and skills that require additional practice.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple and interactive interface for a better user experience.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **User Input:** The user provides the required interview details and skills.
2. **Interview Generation:** The AI generates relevant interview questions.
3. **Mock Interview:** The user attempts to answer the generated questions.
4. **Response Analysis:** The AI evaluates the submitted answers.
5. **Skill Gap Identification:** The system identifies knowledge gaps and weak areas.
6. **Feedback & Recommendations:** The user receives feedback and suggestions for improvement.
